Company Overview

Vector Trade Capital LLC is a United States commodity trading company incorporated in Texas and headquartered in Houston. The company originates, structures, and executes bulk commodity transactions — including petroleum products, food staples, and vehicles — for buyers, sellers, and qualified counterparties across the Caribbean and emerging markets.

Where VTC acts as principal, it takes title to goods, deploys its own capital for procurement, arranges ocean freight and marine cargo insurance, and delivers cargo CIF to the buyer's named port. For larger or mandate-based transactions, VTC structures bankable payment mechanisms and connects qualified counterparties through fee-protected origination.

The company maintains an affiliated entity, Vector Antilles Management Consultancies FZCO, registered in Dubai Silicon Oasis, UAE, supporting Middle East and international trade operations. Vehicle exports are handled through Vector Auto Exports LLC.

Business Lines

Fuel & Energy

Refined petroleum products including ULSD diesel, EN590, gasoline, Jet A-1, LPG, heavy fuel oil, and marine gas oil. Sourced from US Gulf Coast refineries and delivered via tanker, ISO tanks, or flexitank. See fuel supply page.

Food Commodities

Bulk food staples including parboiled rice, ICUMSA 45 sugar, soybean and palm cooking oil, wheat flour, frozen poultry, and dried legumes. Graded to USDA FAS export standards. See food commodity page.

Vehicles & Equipment

New and used vehicles, commercial fleet units, and parts sourced from US dealers and auctions. Exported FOB or CIF with full compliance documentation. See vehicle export page.

How VTC Participates

  • Principal Trader — VTC buys, sells, and executes on its own account
  • CIF Supply Partner — sources, finances, ships, insures, and documents cargo
  • Buyer Representative — helps qualified buyers source from verified suppliers
  • Seller Origination Partner — connects producers and refiners with qualified buyers
  • Fee-Protected Introducer — introduces counterparties under written commercial protection
  • Compliance Coordinator — KYC, sanctions screening, documentation, inspection

Regulatory & Compliance Status

Vector Trade Capital LLC is a fully registered US company with active export authorizations. All transactions comply with the Export Administration Regulations (EAR), OFAC sanctions programs, and applicable US trade regulations.

  • OFAC License: Active — authorizes transactions in designated markets
  • BIS Export License: Active — covers dual-use commodities
  • TSREEA Authorization: Active — food/agriculture authorization
  • US Exporter Registration: Active with Census Bureau AES

Restricted-market transactions are executed exclusively through licensed structures. See restricted markets policy.

Geographic Presence

Houston provides direct access to US Gulf Coast refineries, agricultural producers, and the Port of Houston. Miami supports Caribbean logistics and buyer relationships. Dubai supports Middle East and international trade corridors.

Active supply markets: Jamaica, Dominican Republic, Trinidad & Tobago, Bahamas, Guyana, Barbados, Haiti, Saint Lucia, Belize, Suriname, and Panama.

Trade Finance

Where VTC acts as principal, it deploys its own capital to purchase, ship, and insure cargo. For larger or mandate-based transactions, VTC structures bankable payment mechanisms. Supported instruments:

  • Letters of credit (LC)
  • Standby letters of credit (SBLC)
  • Documents against acceptance (DA)
  • Escrow and document-triggered settlement
  • Open account for established buyers

Terms aligned with ICC Incoterms® Rules and international trade finance best practices.
