Company Overview

Vector Trade Capital LLC is a United States commodity trading company incorporated in Texas and headquartered in Houston. The company sources, finances, and delivers bulk petroleum products, food commodities, and vehicles to wholesale importers across the Caribbean and Latin America. Vector Trade Capital operates on a principal-trader model: it takes title to goods, finances procurement with its own balance sheet, arranges ocean freight and marine cargo insurance, and delivers cargo CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight) to the buyer's named destination port.

The company's operational model eliminates broker intermediaries from the supply chain. Rather than acting as an agent that connects buyers and sellers, Vector Trade Capital purchases product directly from producers and refineries — primarily along the US Gulf Coast — and sells directly to end-market importers in the Caribbean. This structure gives Caribbean buyers a single, capital-backed counterparty and removes the counterparty risk and timing delays associated with broker-arranged transactions.

Vector Trade Capital maintains an affiliated entity, Vector Antilles Management Consultancies FZCO, registered in Dubai Silicon Oasis, UAE, which supports Middle East and international trade operations. The company also operates Vector Auto Exports LLC, a US-registered vehicle export entity.

Business Lines

Fuel & Energy

Vector Trade Capital supplies refined petroleum products to Caribbean fuel terminals, power generators, airlines, and marine operators. Products include ultra-low sulfur diesel (ULSD), EN590 diesel, gasoline (87/89/93 octane), Jet A-1 aviation fuel, LPG propane, heavy fuel oil (HFO 180/380 cst), and marine gas oil (MGO). All petroleum products are sourced from US Gulf Coast refineries and priced against published benchmark indices. Delivery is executed via chartered tankers, ISO tanks, or flexitanks depending on volume. See fuel supply page.

Food Commodities

The company exports bulk food staples to Caribbean distributors, government agencies, and retailers. Commodity lines include parboiled long-grain rice, ICUMSA 45 refined white sugar, soybean and palm cooking oil, all-purpose wheat flour, IQF frozen poultry (chicken leg quarters and whole birds), and dried legumes (black beans, kidney beans, chickpeas). Products are sourced from US producers and South American exporters, graded and inspected to USDA Foreign Agricultural Service export standards, and delivered in 20-foot or 40-foot containers with full phytosanitary and certificate of origin documentation. See food commodity page.

Vehicles & Equipment

Vector Trade Capital sources new and used passenger vehicles, commercial fleet units, and automotive parts from US dealerships and wholesale auctions for export to Caribbean markets. Vehicles are exported FOB US port or CIF buyer's named Caribbean port, with full US export compliance documentation including BIS export authorization where required. See vehicle export page.

Regulatory & Compliance Status

Vector Trade Capital LLC is a fully registered US company with active export authorizations. All transactions are conducted in compliance with applicable US government regulations, including the Export Administration Regulations (EAR) administered by the Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS), OFAC sanctions programs administered by the US Treasury Office of Foreign Assets Control, and the Trading with the Enemy Act/Cuban Assets Control Regulations (CACR) where applicable. Transactions involving restricted markets are executed exclusively under applicable US government licenses and in coordination with licensed trade partners.

  • OFAC License: Active — authorizes transactions in designated markets under applicable exemptions
  • BIS Export License: Active — covers dual-use commodities and controlled goods
  • TSREEA Authorization: Active — Trade Sanctions Reform and Export Enhancement Act food/agriculture authorization
  • US Exporter Registration: Active with US Census Bureau Automated Export System (AES)

For transactions involving markets subject to US trade restrictions, Vector Trade Capital operates exclusively through its licensed structure and does not execute unauthorized transactions. Prospective buyers in restricted markets should review the company's restricted markets policy page.

Geographic Presence

Vector Trade Capital's primary operational hub is Houston, Texas, which provides direct access to US Gulf Coast petroleum refineries, agricultural producers, and port infrastructure including the Port of Houston, one of the largest ports in the United States by total tonnage. The company maintains a trade operations presence in Miami, Florida, supporting Caribbean-focused logistics and buyer relationships. An international affiliate entity is registered in Dubai Silicon Oasis, UAE, supporting Middle East and international trade corridors.

Active supply markets include Jamaica, the Dominican Republic, Trinidad & Tobago, the Bahamas, Guyana, Barbados, Haiti, Saint Lucia, Belize, Suriname, and Panama. The company is actively expanding into additional Caribbean and Latin American markets.

Trade Finance Model

Unlike commodity brokers who require buyer pre-payment before sourcing product, Vector Trade Capital deploys its own working capital to purchase, ship, and insure each cargo before delivery. This self-financed principal trading model means:

  • Buyers deal with a single counterparty that owns the goods throughout the transaction
  • No dependency on third-party funding timelines or broker counterparty sourcing
  • Flexible payment structures supported: letters of credit (LC), documents against acceptance (DA), and open account for established buyers
  • CIF pricing is fixed and firm — freight, insurance, and origin cost are bundled into a single delivered price
  • Quote turnaround: 48 hours for a firm CIF price to any named Caribbean port

Payment terms and financing structures are aligned with ICC Incoterms® Rules and international trade finance best practices.
