Trinidad & Tobago's Import Market

Mercado de Importación de Trinidad y Tobago

Despite its energy wealth, Trinidad and Tobago imports the vast majority of its food supply. The country's food import bill exceeds $1 billion annually, with rice, poultry, cooking oil, and sugar among the top categories. The twin-island nation's food service sector, supermarket chains, and institutional buyers — including the military, hospitals, and schools — drive consistent demand for bulk food commodities. Trinidad's position as a CARICOM trade hub also makes it a distribution point for smaller Eastern Caribbean islands. The Port of Port of Spain handles containerized cargo while Point Lisas Industrial Estate serves as the primary fuel and bulk commodity terminal. Vector Trade Capital ships directly from Houston and Miami, with transit times of 7-10 days to Port of Spain.

A pesar de su riqueza energética, Trinidad y Tobago importa la gran mayoría de su suministro alimenticio. La factura anual de importación de alimentos supera los $1 mil millones. La posición de Trinidad como centro comercial de CARICOM también la convierte en punto de distribución para islas más pequeñas del Caribe Oriental.
