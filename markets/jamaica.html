Jamaica's Import Landscape

Panorama de Importaciones de Jamaica

With a population of nearly 3 million, Jamaica relies on imported food for approximately 60% of its national consumption. Rice, wheat flour, sugar, cooking oil, and frozen poultry are among the most imported food categories. The Jamaican petroleum sector is entirely import-dependent, with diesel and gasoline arriving primarily from US Gulf Coast refineries via established Caribbean fuel supply chains. The Port of Kingston — one of the Caribbean's largest transshipment hubs — handles the bulk of these imports efficiently, while Montego Bay serves the island's western parishes. We also serve importers across the Dominican Republic, Trinidad & Tobago, the Bahamas, and Guyana.

Con una población de casi 3 millones, Jamaica depende de alimentos importados para aproximadamente el 60% de su consumo nacional. El arroz, la harina de trigo, el azúcar, el aceite de cocina y el pollo congelado se encuentran entre las categorías más importadas. El sector petrolero jamaiquino depende totalmente de las importaciones, con diésel y gasolina provenientes principalmente de refinerías de la Costa del Golfo de EE.UU.

Port of Kingston — Import Terminal Operations

Puerto de Kingston — Operaciones de Terminal de Importación

The Port of Kingston is Jamaica's primary commercial gateway and the Caribbean's seventh-largest natural harbor. Kingston Freeport Terminal (KFT) handles over 1.8 million TEUs annually, making it the busiest transshipment port in the English-speaking Caribbean. For fuel importers, Kingston's Petrojam terminal at Jackson Bay receives ULSD diesel and gasoline via tanker from U.S. Gulf Coast refineries. As a diesel supplier for Jamaica, Vector Trade Capital delivers CIF to Kingston with 4–7 day transit from Houston, providing consistent supply to fuel distributors, power generators, and industrial consumers across the island.

El Puerto de Kingston es la principal puerta comercial de Jamaica y el séptimo puerto natural más grande del Caribe. La terminal Petrojam en Jackson Bay recibe diésel ULSD y gasolina por tanquero desde refinerías de la Costa del Golfo. Como proveedor de diésel para Jamaica, entregamos CIF a Kingston con tránsito de 4–7 días desde Houston.

For containerized food imports — rice, sugar, cooking oil, and frozen poultry — Kingston Freeport Terminal's modern gantry cranes and reefer plug capacity ensure efficient handling of both dry and temperature-controlled cargo. Montego Bay's port facility serves Jamaica's western parishes and the tourism corridor, handling supplemental food imports for the island's hotel and resort sector. Vector Trade Capital coordinates with established shipping lines on direct routes from New Orleans and Houston to both Jamaican ports.

Para importaciones de alimentos contenedorizados, las grúas modernas y capacidad de conexión para reefer de la Terminal de Kingston aseguran manejo eficiente de carga seca y refrigerada. El puerto de Montego Bay sirve a las parroquias occidentales de Jamaica.
