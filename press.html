Vector Trade Capital Expands Caribbean Commodity Supply Operations with Delivery and Trade Finance

Vector Trade Capital Amplía Operaciones de Suministro de Materias Primas en el Caribe con Entrega y Financiamiento Comercial

Vector Trade Capital expands its Caribbean commodity supply operations, offering integrated delivery and trade finance solutions for food staples, fuel, and vehicles across the region.

Vector Trade Capital amplía sus operaciones de suministro de materias primas en el Caribe, ofreciendo soluciones integradas de entrega y financiamiento comercial para alimentos básicos, combustible y vehículos en toda la región.

Houston, Texas — March 22, 2026

Vector Trade Capital, a U.S.-based commodity supply and logistics firm, today announced the continued expansion of its operations across Caribbean markets, providing bulk fuel and food commodities to importers through a fully integrated CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight) delivery model.

The company supports wholesale importers, fuel distributors, and government buyers across key markets including Jamaica, the Dominican Republic, Trinidad & Tobago, the Bahamas, and Guyana. Vector Trade Capital supplies refined petroleum products such as diesel (ULSD), gasoline, and jet fuel, alongside essential food commodities including rice, frozen poultry, cooking oil, and sugar.

Vector Trade Capital’s model is built around end-to-end execution, managing sourcing, logistics, inspection, and delivery to the destination port. By operating as a principal in transactions and deploying internal capital, the company enables faster execution timelines and reduces reliance on intermediaries, allowing buyers to secure supply with greater certainty.

“Importers across the Caribbean face ongoing challenges around reliability, timing, and access to capital,” said Cole Kutschinski, President of Vector Trade Capital. “Our focus is to simplify that process.”

The company sources primarily from U.S. Gulf Coast suppliers and structures transactions with third-party inspection and full export documentation, ensuring compliance and product integrity across shipments. Vector Trade Capital is designed to respond quickly to market demand, providing CIF pricing within 48 hours and executing transactions within standard commercial timelines.

As demand for both fuel and essential food commodities remains strong across the region, Vector Trade Capital continues to build long-term supply relationships with importers seeking dependable partners capable of delivering at scale.

In addition to its core supply operations, the company has developed structured trade finance capabilities to support importers navigating working capital constraints, further strengthening its role as a comprehensive supply partner in the region.

About Vector Trade Capital

Acerca de Vector Trade Capital

Vector Trade Capital LLC is a Houston-based commodity trading company specializing in the export of food staples, fuel, and vehicles to the Caribbean and Latin America. Operating from Houston and Miami, VTC delivers bulk commodities CIF with integrated trade finance — sourcing directly from US Gulf Coast producers and managing the full supply chain from procurement through port delivery. The company serves qualified trade buyers and wholesale importers across the region.

Vector Trade Capital LLC es una empresa de comercio de materias primas con sede en Houston, especializada en la exportación de alimentos básicos, combustible y vehículos al Caribe y América Latina. Operando desde Houston y Miami, VTC entrega materias primas a granel CIF con financiamiento comercial integrado — abasteciéndose directamente de productores de la Costa del Golfo de EE.UU. y gestionando toda la cadena de suministro desde la adquisición hasta la entrega en puerto.
