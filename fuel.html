Inicio/Combustible y Energía

Vector Trade Capital sources and delivers refined petroleum products to importers, fuel distributors, and power utilities across the Caribbean and beyond. With offices in Houston, Miami, and Dubai, we leverage direct refinery relationships and established shipping routes to deliver consistent, competitively priced fuel supply.

Vector Trade Capital abastece y entrega productos de petróleo refinado a importadores, distribuidores de combustible y empresas eléctricas en todo el Caribe y más allá. Con oficinas en Houston, Miami y Dubái, aprovechamos relaciones directas con refinerías y rutas de envío establecidas para entregar suministro de combustible consistente y a precios competitivos.

Capital-Backed SupplySuministro Respaldado por Capital

Vector Trade Capital operates as a capital-backed fuel supplier for the Caribbean market. Unlike traditional fuel brokers who rely on back-to-back trades, we deploy our own capital to procure ULSD diesel, gasoline, Jet A-1, LPG, heavy fuel oil, and marine gas oil directly from U.S. Gulf Coast refineries and established international suppliers. This trade finance model allows Caribbean fuel importers to secure reliable supply without the complexity of letters of credit or pre-funded purchases, making us a trusted bulk fuel supplier for the Caribbean.

As a Houston-based ULSD supplier for the Caribbean, we leverage our proximity to major refining centers along the Gulf Coast to offer competitive freight rates and rapid delivery to every major Caribbean terminal. Our capital-backed approach means we manage the entire fuel supply chain — refinery procurement, independent quality inspection, vessel nomination, marine insurance, and freight — delivering a fully managed CIF solution to your terminal.

Whether you are a national fuel importer, a power generation company, or an aviation fuel distributor, our trade finance model provides supply certainty on predictable schedules. Contact us to learn how our capital-backed fuel supply model can support your energy import requirements across the Caribbean.
