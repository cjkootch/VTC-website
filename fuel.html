CIF Fuel Supplier Caribbean

As a CIF fuel supplier Caribbean importers rely on, Vector Trade Capital manages the complete supply chain from refinery gate to your terminal. CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight) delivery means the landed price you receive includes the product cost, marine cargo insurance, ocean freight, and independent quality inspection at the load port. This turnkey approach makes us the preferred CIF fuel supplier for Caribbean ports including Kingston, Caucedo, Nassau, Port of Spain, and Georgetown — eliminating the logistical complexity that importers would otherwise need to manage themselves.

What CIF Fuel Delivery Includes

  • Product procurement from U.S. Gulf Coast refineries at competitive benchmark pricing
  • Independent quality and quantity inspection (SGS or equivalent) at the load port
  • Ocean freight on vetted clean petroleum tankers with appropriate vessel size for your port
  • Marine cargo insurance covering the full shipment value from load port to discharge
  • Complete shipping documentation — bill of lading, certificate of quality, certificate of quantity, certificate of origin
  • Customs and compliance documentation meeting destination country import requirements

For Caribbean fuel importers, CIF delivery reduces risk and simplifies procurement. Instead of coordinating separately with refineries, vessel brokers, insurance providers, and inspection agencies, you work with a single fuel supplier — Vector Trade Capital — who manages every step. Learn more about CIF delivery terms in our Caribbean Import Playbook.

Entrega CIF de Combustible a Puertos del Caribe

Como proveedor de combustible CIF para el Caribe, Vector Trade Capital gestiona la cadena de suministro completa desde la refinería hasta su terminal. La entrega CIF (Costo, Seguro y Flete) significa que el precio que recibe incluye el costo del producto, seguro de carga marítima, flete oceánico e inspección de calidad independiente en el puerto de carga.

Para importadores de combustible del Caribe, la entrega CIF reduce el riesgo y simplifica la adquisición.
