Does Vector Trade Capital export vehicles to the Caribbean? Vector Trade Capital exporta vehiculos al Caribe?

Yes. We source new and used vehicles from US dealers and auctions, and export them to Caribbean markets with full compliance documentation. We handle sedans, SUVs, pickup trucks, commercial fleet vehicles, and automotive parts.

Si. Adquirimos vehiculos nuevos y usados de distribuidores y subastas de EE.UU., y los exportamos a mercados del Caribe con documentacion completa de cumplimiento.

How are vehicles shipped to the Caribbean? Como se envian los vehiculos al Caribe?

Vehicles are shipped via RoRo (Roll-on/Roll-off) or container shipping to Caribbean ports. RoRo is typically used for driveable vehicles, while containers are used for parts and vehicles that require enclosed transport.

Los vehiculos se envian mediante RoRo (Roll-on/Roll-off) o contenedor a puertos del Caribe. RoRo se usa para vehiculos que pueden conducirse, mientras que los contenedores se usan para piezas y vehiculos que requieren transporte cerrado.

Can I order fleet vehicles in bulk? Puedo ordenar vehiculos de flota al por mayor?

Yes. We handle fleet orders including commercial trucks, vans, buses, and company vehicles. We source from US dealers, auctions, and fleet liquidation sales, providing competitive pricing for bulk vehicle export.

Si. Manejamos pedidos de flotas que incluyen camiones comerciales, furgonetas, autobuses y vehiculos de empresa, con precios competitivos para exportacion a granel.

What documentation is provided with vehicle exports? Que documentacion se proporciona con las exportaciones de vehiculos?

All vehicle exports include title documentation, bill of sale, export declarations, bills of lading, and compliance with US export regulations. We handle all paperwork to ensure smooth customs clearance at the destination port.

Todas las exportaciones incluyen documentacion de titulo, factura de venta, declaraciones de exportacion, conocimientos de embarque y cumplimiento con regulaciones de exportacion de EE.UU.

Which Caribbean countries can you export vehicles to? A que paises del Caribe pueden exportar vehiculos?

We export vehicles to importers and dealers across the Caribbean including Jamaica, Trinidad & Tobago, Dominican Republic, Bahamas, Barbados, Guyana, Haiti, and the wider Caribbean Basin. Each destination has specific import regulations — our team handles the compliance requirements for each country to ensure smooth customs clearance at the destination port.

Exportamos vehiculos a importadores y distribuidores en todo el Caribe, incluyendo Jamaica, Trinidad y Tobago, Republica Dominicana, Bahamas, Barbados, Guyana, Haiti y toda la cuenca del Caribe. Nuestro equipo maneja los requisitos de cumplimiento para cada pais.

Do you offer financing for vehicle exports? Ofrecen financiamiento para exportaciones de vehiculos?

Vector Trade Capital is a capital-backed trading platform. We finance vehicle procurement and export logistics with our own capital, which means faster execution and no third-party broker delays. For qualifying buyers with established import operations, we can structure flexible payment terms on bulk fleet orders. Contact us to discuss your requirements.

Vector Trade Capital es una plataforma de comercio respaldada por capital. Financiamos la adquisicion y logistica de exportacion de vehiculos con nuestro propio capital. Para compradores calificados, podemos estructurar terminos de pago flexibles en pedidos de flotas. Contactenos para discutir sus necesidades.

What is the difference between FOB and CIF for vehicle export? Cual es la diferencia entre FOB y CIF para la exportacion de vehiculos?

FOB (Free on Board) means we deliver the vehicle to the US port of export — you arrange ocean freight and insurance. CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight) means we handle everything: procurement, inland transport to port, ocean freight, and marine cargo insurance to your destination port. Most Caribbean buyers prefer CIF for the simplicity of a single landed price. Learn more about how CIF delivery works.

FOB (Franco a Bordo) significa que entregamos el vehiculo en el puerto de exportacion de EE.UU. CIF (Costo, Seguro y Flete) significa que manejamos todo: adquisicion, transporte al puerto, flete oceanico y seguro de carga maritima hasta su puerto de destino. La mayoria de los compradores caribenos prefieren CIF por la simplicidad de un precio unico. Aprenda mas sobre como funciona la entrega CIF.
